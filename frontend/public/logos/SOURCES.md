# Brand SVG sources

Most marks are extracted from the `simple-icons` and `simple-icons-modern` npm packages; see their included license and trademark guidance. Legacy Microsoft brands are retained from Simple Icons 11.15.0.

- LlamaIndex: `@lobehub/icons-static-svg/icons/llamaindex.svg`, exported into `src/logo-paths.json`.
- Pinecone: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/callback-io/allogo@main/public/logos/pinecone/icon.svg
- Weaviate: https://weaviate.io/img/site/2026/weaviate-logo-2-colours-dark-green.svg (the standalone symbol extracted from the official wordmark; rendered in green).
- Chroma: https://www.trychroma.com/_next/static/immutable/media/chroma-wordmark.3omc55d6a9e1l.svg (the standalone symbol extracted from the official wordmark).

Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners and are used to identify technologies.
